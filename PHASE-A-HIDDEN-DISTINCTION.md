## A.2 Candidate distinctions (5)

### Candidate 1 — The Only Strategy + Execution Integration with KLAS-Validated Execution
- **The fact:** The combined entity is the only HIT services firm that brings 22-year strategic advisory expertise (ICG's legacy, founded 2002) AND 4-consecutive-year #1 KLAS Managed IT Services execution (Pivot Point's legacy, 2023-2026) under one accountability structure.
- **Public source:** PR Newswire announcement; ICG's pre-merger profile; KLAS public rankings.
- **Why it's buried:** Both current websites talk about the merger but don't articulate the structural advantage as the spine. The current Pivot Point hero is still "Move Faster, Make Decisions with Confidence" — generic transformation-speak.
- **Why it's irreducible:** No other firm has this combination. Big-Four firms have advisory but lack KLAS-validated managed services execution. Mid-market managed services firms (Galen, Avaap, Optimum) lack 22-year advisory pedigree. Strategy boutiques (Chartis) don't run managed services. The integration is uniquely Pivot Point + ICG.

### Candidate 2 — Closing the Strategy-to-Execution Gap
- **The fact:** Most HIT transformations fail at the handoff between strategy (consulting firm A) and execution (firm B or in-house team). The combined Pivot Point eliminates that handoff by putting both under one P&L.
- **Public source:** Inferred from the PE thesis + investor rationale in the announcement.
- **Why it's buried:** No firm has positioned around the strategy-execution gap; it's a real buyer pain that lives below the surface.
- **Why it's irreducible:** A merged firm with both capabilities can claim this; firms that only do one side cannot.

### Candidate 3 — 26 Years of Compounding Healthcare IT Expertise
- **The fact:** 22 years of ICG strategic advisory + 14+ years of Pivot Point execution = 26+ years of healthcare IT under one roof. Plus 10+ years of consecutive KLAS recognition.
- **Public source:** ICG founded 2002 (LeadIQ/ZoomInfo); Pivot Point founded 2011 (their about page); KLAS history 2015-2026.
- **Why it's buried:** Time-based credibility is unmodified by the merger.
- **Why it might not be strongest:** Pure tenure isn't ownable in the same way the structural integration is. Many firms can claim decades of experience.

### Candidate 4 — Vendor-Agnostic AND KLAS-Validated
- **The fact:** ICG was vendor-agnostic (works with all EMR/EHR systems per LeadIQ). Pivot Point is platform-broad (Epic, Cerner, MEDITECH, ERP). Combined entity has no platform conflict-of-interest AND #1 KLAS validation.
- **Public source:** ICG's pre-merger profile + Pivot Point's platform pages.
- **Why it might not be strongest:** Vendor-agnostic is a common HIT consulting claim. The KLAS streak is more uniquely defensible alone.

### Candidate 5 — Built for Healthcare's Structural Reset
- **The fact:** The PE investment thesis names the macro shift the merged firm is built for: workforce constraints, cost pressures, regulatory complexity, accelerating digital adoption.
- **Public source:** Investor announcement language.
- **Why it might not be strongest:** Every HIT firm could plausibly claim this macro positioning. It's a category-wide narrative, not a firm-specific irreducible truth.
